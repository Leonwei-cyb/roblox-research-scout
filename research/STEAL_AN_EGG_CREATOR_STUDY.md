## Game DNA (the publicly documented core, not firsthand gameplay analysis)

The game's official description specifies:

1. **Risk/action:** run to take an egg from pets, evade pursuit and theft from others.
2. **Reward/reveal:** hatch into pets with different rarity, size and mutations.
3. **Passive income:** owned pets generate money.
4. **Reinvestment:** upgrade treadmill/speed and base to reach more valuable zones, repeat.
5. **Meta/social layer:** rare collections, steals from other players, developer-run events and biomes.

**Design hypothesis to test against footage:** the escape/transport risk is the moment-to-moment hook, while hatching rarity + cash production bankroll repeat attempts. Verify exactly when players get first income and their first meaningful upgrade rather than guessing.

## The observations we want a reviewer to capture from a real gameplay/video session

A **5–10 minute consecutive segment** with timestamps:

- First 15 seconds: avatar spawn, camera, obvious action and goal.
- First 30–120 seconds: egg theft, enemy reaction and carry/escape animation; what makes that physically satisfying?
- First hatching/pet: reveal anticipation, rarity legibility, monetized payout amount and next action.
- First speed/base purchase: actual visible gameplay change (not just number growth).
- Later progression: how new biomes/mutations/events change the SAME repeated loop.
- Simple 3D/GUI choices worth learning from (bright Roblox Parts, critical feedback, limited clutter) without copying protected assets.

For every finding record: `timestamp → actually visible event → why it affects game feel → legal/reusable principle`. Label textual articles and screenshots separate from watched video.
